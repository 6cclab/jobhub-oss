# Communication Style Notes

> This file captures your natural writing voice so that Claude can match your tone when drafting application answers, cover letters, and other text that should sound like you.

## How to Generate This File

The best way to populate this is to paste 5-10 samples of your real writing (Slack messages, emails, chat conversations) and ask Claude to extract your communication patterns. Look for:

- **Registers**: Do you write differently in different contexts? (work Slack vs. casual chat vs. formal docs)
- **Sentence structure**: Short and punchy? Long and flowing? Fragments?
- **Tone markers**: Dry humor? Earnest? Direct? Warm?
- **What you never do**: Emoji usage, exclamation points, filler phrases you avoid
