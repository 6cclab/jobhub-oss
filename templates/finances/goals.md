# Goals

Targets and progress. Read and updated by `/finance-plan`; reported on every run, not just when
you ask for `goals`.

## The rules

- **A percentage without a landing date is not progress tracking.** Every goal carries the date it
  lands at the current contribution rate.
- **A goal with no contribution rate is not at 0% progress.** It is stalled, which is a different
  finding and gets said differently.
- **Goals come from you.** `/finance-plan` does not invent targets from what seems prudent.

## Format

```markdown
## <name>
- **Target:** $0 by YYYY-MM-DD (or "no deadline")
- **Now:** $0 — source, actual or estimate
- **Rate:** $0/month
- **Lands:** YYYY-MM-DD at the current rate
- **Why:** one line, so a later read knows whether it still matters
```
