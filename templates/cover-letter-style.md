# Cover Letter Style Guide

Use this as the tone/structure template for all cover letters. Calibrate it against your best cover letter — one where the tone landed and you'd send it again.

## Structure

1. **Opening hook** — Name the specific role and team. Call out one thing from the job posting that resonated and connect it to your experience in one sentence. No generic "I'm excited to apply" openers.

2. **Core paragraph (product/technical)** — Pick the 2-3 most relevant accomplishments and describe them conversationally, not as bullet points. Use specific details (numbers, system names, user counts) but embed them in narrative. Connect your work to the company's problem domain — "It's the same shape as what your team does."

3. **Metrics paragraph** — Lead with "I'm a metrics person" or similar framing. Pick 2-3 concrete numbers. Then pivot to a current/recent initiative that's relevant to the role (experimentation, growth, etc.).

4. **Leadership paragraph** — Technical direction, mentoring, cross-team influence. Mention teaching background or similar framing for how you think about the role, if relevant.

5. **Honest gap acknowledgment** — Be upfront about what you haven't done ("I'll be upfront: I haven't worked on X at Y scale"). Then immediately counter with adjacent experience and learning speed. End with confidence, not apology.

6. **Close** — One sentence. "Happy to dig into any of this further. Thanks for your time."

## Tone

- Conversational but substantive — reads like a smart colleague explaining their work, not a formal letter
- First person, active voice throughout
- No buzzwords or filler ("passionate about," "leveraging," "synergies")
- Specific over general — name the systems, the numbers, the teams
- Confident without being arrogant — "I know how to work with ML engineers to get model outputs into production" not "I am an expert in ML"
- Honest about gaps — this builds credibility for the claims you do make

## Length

~4-5 paragraphs, fits on one page. Use your best cover letter as the reference for ideal length and pacing.

## Formatting

- Name, email, phone, location, LinkedIn, GitHub at the top
- Date
- "Dear Hiring Team," (not "Hi," — too casual)
- Sign off with just your first name
